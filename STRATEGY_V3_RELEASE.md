## Connected research loop

Ordinary theorem development is the default. A researcher may investigate an
uncertainty using literature, computations, a representation change or a new
interface. Prospective commitments and their versions precede the action.
Actual outcomes inform the next request. Typed component admissions retain their
premises, exact source context and checked transport laws; composition checks
require actual declaration dependencies.

The combined arm can compare an incumbent and proposed request program over
matched multiround continuations. Independent evaluation and review can change
the program used in subsequent requests. Researcher discoveries from either
continuation enter a source-bound pending inbox before selection. They require
an ordinary, paid, fresh main-run proof check before admission. Evaluator answers
do not enter that inbox. Delayed usefulness is observable rather than a demand
that every lemma immediately solve the root.

This is an empirical strategy mechanism coupled to checked mathematical models.
The Lean pushout and continuation theorems do not certify arbitrary changes to
the Python researcher. D1/D2/D3 remain open. There is no implemented model
category or general conservative linker for arbitrary dependent Lean modules.
Complexity is audit-only, including route-description overhead and unknown
minimum complexity. No action reward, theorem-count score or prescribed sawtooth
is used.
