# Machine-owned mission

The user explicitly requires the machine itself to rebuild the machinery and
perform the mathematical campaigns. The supervisor supplies requirements,
resource boundaries, primary-paper identities, independent review, and versioning;
it must not supply mathematical answers or prewritten proof recipes.

## Construction

Read docs/DESIGN.md in full. Starting from this empty project, implement the
research system and its formal foundations. The actual proposer is Codex with
gpt-6-astra. Develop executable code, not just an architectural report. Keep
problem contracts explicit, and separate mathematical truth from usefulness
and novelty. There are no artificial research levels or lemma-count rewards.
Kolmogorov/description-length measures are audit-only and may not affect
curiosity, scheduling, admission, or switch approval.

The action space must support literature search, Python/Sage/GAP experiments
when available, representation changes, conjectures, Lean proofs/refutations,
component gluing, and proposed code changes. Unavailable tools are capability
failures. Experiments and retrieved text are not Lean theorems. Provisional
exploration remains flexible; persistent certified self-rewrites require the
whole-continuation proof, an operational connection, exact bindings, and costs.

Construct and test the proof-admission boundary and the reflective execution
model. Formally define the gluing maps and prove the preservation properties
actually used. Demonstrate a concrete installed self-rewrite tied to its checked
semantics. Explicitly report model assumptions and implementation limits.
Do not call a benchmark improvement, abstract implication, changed prompt, or
untested gate a full Gödel machine. Never introduce sorry, admissions, hidden
axioms, native-evaluation axioms, or a postulated global self-soundness theorem.
Use ordinary Mathlib axioms only, and independently audit English versus Lean.

failed attempts and explicit remaining obligations. The supervisor will make
