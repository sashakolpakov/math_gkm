## Connected behavior

- A proposer commits exact closed prediction statements before development.
  Their immutable method-plan registry is included in the prospective binding.
- Ordinary Lean admission precedes optional candidate packaging. Malformed
  method metadata cannot roll back checked mathematics.
- A controller-selected frozen predecessor is extended only with selected
  admitted source closures and new application notes. Policy and old files stay
  exact. Source-file granularity can include accompanying declarations.
- The prediction gate parses targets in the predecessor context with implicit
  free variables disabled, then checks the selected new proofs against them.
- Method, interim-assessment and final holdouts occupy disjoint pretrial pools.
  Used method batches retire; checked applications become private regressions.
- Repeated fresh comparisons balance adapter order, check selected-export use,
  and charge calls, validation and reviews to the owning allowance. Nested
  parent/child call reservations conserve the allocation.
- Method aggregates and measured costs reach actual next proposer/researcher
  requests. Hidden targets, identities, solutions and diagnostics do not.
- Final assessment resources are protected in both arms. Insufficient live
  challenge pools are rejected before expensive deployment work.
- Invalid or missing comparisons remain inconclusive. Bounded predecessor
  failure is not unprovability; dependency use is not causal indispensability.
  Description-length measurements remain audit-only.
