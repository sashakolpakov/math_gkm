## Continuing beyond a finite research period

The source in `.bootstrap/method-learning-gaussian-20261103/continuation/` is
outside the sealed native implementation. A registered live wrapper must first
observe the numeric exit of its own native controller. The new owner waits for
that exact closure; a stale status, saved process identifier or elapsed time
cannot establish closure. Native recovery is permitted only for closed jobs with
numeric receipts, followed by the ordinary native verification.

A completed finite period with the root still open can lead to another funded
period. Migration retains the same research history, checked mathematics,
components, concepts, pending discoveries, method plans and feedback. Previous
costs remain recorded. The original clean predecessor snapshot remains distinct
from the inherited theory at the start of a successor. Bound proof records are
verified before and after relocation. Required historical evidence stays
available in its original location; it must not be deleted as compilation debris.

Partially started method comparisons cannot be moved onto newly chosen questions.
An unstarted inherited method can face a fresh, independently curated panel; its
record explicitly says selection occurred after method acquisition. The curator
receives the original clean goal and background, without acquired methods,
research artifacts or prior answers. This separation of supplied inputs is not a
claim of statistical independence or universal novelty.

Resource limits, source limits and registry limits remain enforced. The owner can
wait for restored disk/memory headroom. An uncertain operation requires
reconciliation; it cannot be silently replayed or reported as a mathematical
failure. This machinery does not promise that the conjecture is true, decidable
by these methods, or attainable within available resources.

## What can close the proof goal

Counts, bounded examples, sufficient conditions, successful applications and a
finished research period do not resolve the goal. A candidate must pass the
unchanged exact-root Lean elaboration, kernel replay, premise inspection and
bundle checks. A separate independent correspondence reviewer must also explain
every source/English obligation for that exact source bundle. A rejection is a
recorded finding, not permission to replace the target or manufacture acceptance.
A refutation has its own outcome and cannot be labeled a proof.

The native controller currently records an exact Lean proof without resolving it
when an independent audit is missing. The continuation adapter connects that
pending stage to a separate external reviewer and the existing native completion
API. Finalization has a separate bounded allowance: 1,800 seconds for the
independent reviewer and 600 seconds for the unchanged native validator. It
retains a genuine finished completion receipt without modifying the original
arm state or enlarging its allowance. Both costs and provenance remain separate;
no later audit result retroactively changes the original comparison's resource
record.
