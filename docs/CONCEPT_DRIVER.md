# Concept-directed research loop

This revision makes conceptual investigation a scheduled part of the existing
mathematical research loop. It preserves ordinary proof production, exact Lean
admission, checked interfaces, and the original problem contract. It is an
operator-engineered experiment, not a machine-proved improvement of its entire
future continuation. Mathematical proposals and research actions come from the
machine's own separate proposer and researcher invocations.

## A concept is a prospective explanation

A concept plan records competing explanations, one selected mechanism, at least
two facts or obstacles it proposes to explain, a connection to the unchanged
root goal, explicit remaining obligations, and at least two prospective
consequences with stated structural differences. Checkpoints ask what evidence
would change the next choice. The proposer may use literature, numerical or
symbolic experiments, alternative definitions, examples, counterexamples, or
proof work. There is no fixed mathematical tactic curriculum.

These records are conjectural research plans. Distinct strings do not prove
that consequences differ mathematically. A claim labeled proved in a plan is
still reported prose until its exact theorem passes the independent admission
path. Adding quantifiers, naming a definition, or accumulating successful cases
does not automatically establish conceptual progress.

## Connected execution

The actual controller path is:

1. Project the accepted theory, open obligations, competing explanations,
   source review feedback, and the current protected concept episode.
2. The independent proposer commits its plan before any research action.
3. The controller binds the immutable plan and reserves research, comparison,
   and review envelopes through its horizon, including any additional review
   at a concept boundary that differs from the ordinary episode boundary.
4. The separate researcher executes that commitment and submits ordinary Lean
   mathematics together with optional concept observations.
5. Mathematics is admitted independently of optional narrative metadata.
6. At a review, a source-only reviewer diagnoses the arm's own mathematical
   artifacts. Private evaluation tasks and answers are absent from its inputs.
7. The controller persists a bounded review packet and returns it to subsequent
   proposer and researcher requests, with delivery records tied to actual job
   identities. A delivery receipt does not prove the model understood it.

The exploration arm must periodically start a concept episode. An episode may
span several invocations without producing a theorem. An interim review does
not terminate its horizon. Missing observations remain missing evidence.
Ordinary proof work can support the same episode, and ordinary exploitation is
also available between concept episodes. Repeated reported parameter cases
require a prospective explanation of what another case would discriminate.
The baseline arm retains its single revisable agenda and receives the same safe
source-review feedback without mandatory concept episodes.

A concept commitment supplies its own prospective experimental contract; it
need not duplicate the older optional trial/hypothesis portfolio. In finite
strategy-comparison branches, the exact branch end bounds new concept grants.
An inherited longer grant is observed only as an explicitly labeled prefix and
remains unresolved; a finite comparison never certifies its whole continuation.

These are time and resource allocations, not manufactured mathematical levels.
At the end of a finite run, a shorter remaining interval permits ordinary work
rather than forcing an impossible new grant. A completed concept horizon
triggers review before another concept silently replaces it.

## Correctness and mathematical scope

The existing compile, kernel replay, declaration identity, axiom, source,
interface, dependency-use and exact-root checks remain authoritative. A concept
report, reviewer recommendation, successful program exit, or hypothesis label
cannot admit a theorem or close the root. Genuine mathematical negation requires
its own checked witness and exact hypotheses.

Typed attachments and representation/gluing records remain part of ordinary
mathematical submission. A proposed mechanism may supply a common interface
and interpretations of several constructions. Their laws and transport back to
the original predicates require proofs. Compatibility supports reuse; it does
not establish usefulness. The categorical results and the limits of the words
cofibered/cofibrant are documented in [GLUING.md](GLUING.md).

## Prospective comparison and attribution

An independent pretrial curator selects a small suite from the clean task
sources before either research arm runs. The operator supplies no mathematical
lemmas or answers. Suite-size limits concern experiment cost only; oversized
outputs are retained and rejected rather than truncated after inspection.

At a completed concept horizon, the controller compares the actual snapshot
immediately before its start with the snapshot at its end. The exact same
withheld statements, model configuration and adaptation limits are used on both
sides. Frozen declaration checks establish the available mathematical context;
proof dependency inspection distinguishes actual use of new declarations from
new comments, filenames or unused additions. A valid gain requires a checked
new solution, a valid bounded non-solution on the old side, and regression
retention. Bounded failure is not a theorem that the old library cannot solve
that problem.

The measured object is the complete new library package developed during that
interval. A comparison only supports the concept episode when its own reference
and both interval boundaries agree exactly. A normal or misaligned library
comparison cannot support that concept. Even an aligned gain does not establish
that one particular lemma or explanatory sentence was indispensable. Separately
precommitted ablations, where feasible, provide stronger evidence; no ablation
result is fabricated when one is unavailable.

Review feedback contains only numeric/boolean comparison aggregates and typed
measured costs. Private task identifiers, statements, solutions, catalogs,
compiler output and evaluator free text are excluded. The mathematical source
reviewer is given a distinct workspace and job identity, with only main-arm
proposer/researcher receipts and own source/history. A legacy reviewer that saw
withheld material cannot be relabeled source-only on recovery.

## Costs, uncertainty and complexity audit

Development role elapsed time is measured over the compared interval. Adapter,
validation and frozen-context checking costs are recorded separately for each
comparison side. Reservation caps are not measured elapsed time. Unknown costs
stay null, and overlapping controller wall time is not added to subprocess time.

Invalid comparisons, timeouts, output limits, unavailable capabilities and
inspection limits are inconclusive. They never become zero transfer, refuted
mathematics, or negative evidence manufactured against a difficult concept.
Feedback remains an interpretation; it does not automatically change the truth
status of a hypothesis or certify a policy switch.

Description length, compression and Kolmogorov-style measurements retain their
one-way audit role. The concept driver accepts no complexity score. A sawtooth
can be investigated through separately recorded theory investment, application
costs and transfer events. No measurement pretends to know the complexity of an
unfinished proof, and no desired curve is forced into the results.

Schmidhuber's learning-progress idea motivates comparing old and new capability
on the same evidence. This implementation uses prospective mathematical transfer
as empirical evidence, not as a universal measure of mathematical value. The
whole-continuation, executable-refinement and certified-installation obligations
remain open; ordinary exploration does not require those proofs at each action.
See [the creativity paper](https://people.idsia.ch/~juergen/ieeecreative.pdf) and
[the original Goedel-machine paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0309048).

## Operational preservation and reproduction

The rerun starts from the same clean mathematical seed. Previous campaign proofs,
agendas and research history are not imported. Both arms share the new
implementation, model, dependency pins and upper allowances. Heavy workers run
sequentially. Disk, memory, process and output limits are preserved. The sampler handles a child that exits between enumeration and footprint collection only after the kernel confirms absence or the same birth as a zombie; live failures and changed process identities still stop execution.
Sequential order and shared provider conditions limit causal conclusions.

One real deployment preflight is recorded at freeze. Each arm verifies its exact
receipt against current implementation, binary, dependency and resource-profile
identities and performs fresh headroom checks; it does not repeat identical
full Lean controls unnecessarily. Legacy or changed bindings fail closed.

Frozen catalog receipts may be reused only for exactly matching contexts through
the official FINISHED-bound verifier. A different source, pin or inspection
identity cannot hit that cache. Cache lookup costs and original compilation
costs are distinct, and reservations remain charged. Growing contexts may still
exceed a finite verification budget; that outcome stays inconclusive.

Implementation: `machinery/study_concepts.py`,
`machinery/study_research_feedback.py`, and their actual controller/policy calls.
Focused tests cover immutable commitments, protected and final horizons,
comparison attribution, information separation, delivery/recovery, invalid
comparisons, cost accounting, exact receipt reuse and launch bindings. A neutral
fixture exercises the complete loop; fixture success is not a mathematical
research result. The dated launch directory retains exact test and independent
review records before any live run is authorized.
