## Authority and interpretation

User requirements include the independent machine-owned proposer, autonomous action choice, construction by the machine, a serious Goedel-machine foundation, visible cofibrant/cofibered gluing, audit-only Kolmogorov measures, no artificial mathematical levels, broad tools, preservation/reproducibility, four approved exploration/strategy steps, fresh matched comparison, and the latest remaining-goal-relative complexity discussion.

The four approved steps are: competing research hypotheses; mathematical transfer evaluation; delayed benefit over episodes; and strategy changes as objects of fair experiments. Specific data structures, numbers of rounds, choice of fixed-panel coding, or review thresholds are implementation proposals and must be labeled as such. No universal score follows from the discussion. A finite evaluation cannot establish whole-continuation optimality.

The latest complexity proposal is interpreted as the remaining proof burden of a fixed goal relative to acquired knowledge. The goal statement is already known. Shortening its name or introducing an abbreviation is not evidence of easier proof. Measured encodings are computable proxies, not minimal Kolmogorov complexities. The user did not revoke the earlier audit-only constraint.

## Required connected research loop

A versioned exact problem contract enters the actual runner. The runner retains competing hypotheses, asks its own proposer for a prospective trial, records the chosen uncertainty/outcomes and episode horizon before execution, performs the chosen research action, records actual evidence, checks any asserted mathematical result and its premises, checks component attachment and representation transport where used, updates explicit remaining obligations, evaluates transfer/capability retention on a fair comparison, reviews the episode, tests any proposed policy change, records the selection with reasons and costs, then constructs the next request from the updated state. Stop, interruption and recovery must preserve that sequence without fabricating a result or repeating an uncertain action.

The description/proof-burden audit observes the same bound snapshots and costs through a one-way channel. Its values and derived proxies do not enter admission, action selection, scheduling, or policy-change acceptance. Mathematical reductions and checked transfer can be research evidence independently of their audit sizes.

Every edge needs a concrete producer, durable artifact, consumer, invariant and verification example. A callable helper or unused CLI command does not establish that an edge participates in the running loop.
