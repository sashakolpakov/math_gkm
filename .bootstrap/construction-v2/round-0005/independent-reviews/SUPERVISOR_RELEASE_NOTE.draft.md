# Strategy v2: experimental research harness

Version 2 connects research commitments, checked evidence and subsequent decisions. A separate external Codex/gpt-6-astra builder implemented the machinery; supervisory agents reviewed it. The validation runs use deterministic research-role subprocesses and real Lean compilation, kernel replay and premise inspection. They are neutral machinery tests, not a live conjecture campaign.

The connected loop implements four methodological steps:

1. Retain competing research hypotheses and record a trial's uncertainty, expected outcomes, consequences and budget before acting.
2. Compare transfer on the same frozen mathematical targets and contexts, checking actual component use and retained capabilities. Equal allowances are upper caps, not equal measured costs.
3. Allow benefits to appear over prepaid multiround episodes, with retained use links and review at a declared horizon.
4. Treat proposed strategy changes as experiments: compare bounded continuations, retain reviewer/selector evidence, then feed the selected experimental policy or restricted request program into the next actual request.

Typed gluing is visible in the accepted component interfaces, interpretation maps, premises and checked transport laws consumed by later proofs. The formal structures have specific section, factorization and uniqueness laws; they do not establish unrestricted cofibrations, a full model category or the safety of arbitrary source changes.

A one-way observer compares supported reduction-route descriptions for the same goal under old and enlarged accepted theories. It reports residual proof holes and dictionary/adapter/connecting overhead separately, with plots of measured descriptions, costs and changes. Missing points remain missing; no sawtooth or decrease is required. These are audit-only proxies, never rewards or selection inputs. Minimum proof complexity and difficulty remain unknown. Direct root resolution is verified separately; a direct proof's encoding can remain unmeasured.

This is an experimental harness, not a certified Gödel-machine switch. D1—the concrete utility/cost comparison against the whole incumbent continuation—remains open. D2—the connection from the formal model to actual Python/OS/model transitions and migration—remains open. D3—the concrete certificate and independently guarded installation obligations—remains open; the certified-installation path rejects without them.
