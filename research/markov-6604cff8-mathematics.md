## How the matrix bridge was repaired

The prior whole-bivariate gauge expansion was replaced by four generic
coefficient identities in `MarkovGaugeCoeff6604.lean`. For scalar gauges
`a,b` and matrix entries `A,B,C,D`, coefficient k of the gauged entries is
respectively `A_k-B_k*b`, `B_k`, `a*A_k+C_k-(a*B_k+D_k)*b`, and `a*B_k+D_k`.
These are proved over the literal nested integer polynomials.

The 44 required run-three coefficients are extracted from the inherited
exact lower-block matrices before univariate normalization. Four separate
modules verify the sixteen Boolean local-moment cases. A Horner-form table
avoids elaborating one large overloaded expression. The six new chamber
expansions then connect the actual moments with the inherited coefficient
certificates. Multiplication by 24 is canceled uniformly at the end.

## Universal fourth-slice representation

`jet_closed_14N6604` and `jet_closed_34N6604` prove, for every natural n,

    24 (1-X)^9 [U^4] runEntry(e,n) = P_e(n,X) + X^n Q_e(n,X),

for entries e=1 and e=3, which are the upper-right and lower-right entries.
The explicit candidates were generated by exact interpolation and checked
at 42 n-values for each entry; **the universal Lean proofs use two-step
recurrence induction**, not those finite checks. The induction invokes the
actual scalar Cayley-Hamilton recurrence and previously proved third jets.
Two unused generated candidates for the other entries are explicitly excluded.

`MarkovFourthClosedN6604.lean` embeds integer polynomials injectively into
`FractionRing (Polynomial Int)` and transports the first three jets and the
two fourth jets to a closed local fourth moment. The inherited exact Boolean
averaging law supplies the full moment for every predecessor denominator

`closedFourthN6604 n c r` with the embedded actual fourth cut polynomial
multiplied by 24. `source_fourth_closed_iffN6604` proves uniqueness as a whole
integer polynomial; `source_fourth_closed_coeffN6604` extracts the literal
raw coefficients. `source_fourth_lc_of_closedN6604` is an explicit conditional
inequality adapter, whose log-concavity premise remains unproved in general.
Small c=2,3,4 are outside this closed-form theorem, and remain covered only
by the inherited all-cycle parameter formula and the proved fixed-run cases.

No coefficient is evaluated at a number or normalized by its index. The
factor 24 is common to the entire slice. The fraction field is a representation
of polynomial identities, not an extra source-family assumption.

## Experiments, not theorems

`experiments.json` records 684 source/run cases, 3,836 comparisons with literal
raw recurrence coefficients, and 20,760 polygon-valid triples for runs
3,4,7,12 and predecessor denominators 2 through 23. No failure occurred.

`fourth-run-probe.json` records 855 exact symbolic gap expansions for runs
4 through 8, in all six strict Farey chambers. Their coefficients in the
nonnegative chamber coordinates are nonnegative. These are finite-run
algebraic experiments, not Lean source-connected theorems.

`fourth-rational-normalization.json` records an exact Python normalization
of the uniform rational formula. In all six chambers it has denominator
`(1-X)^8`, with powers 0 through 4 of `Z=X^n`. The numerator has 1394 through
1420 terms. Its common Z^0 part has 411 terms and X-degree 12. Each Z^1 part
starts at X-degree 2; higher powers start later. The normalized coefficient
formula passed 1,116 independent comparisons with the cut recurrence.
The composite normalization has **not** been proved in Lean.

`fourth-low-shape.json` derives a common 202-term coefficient polynomial
of degrees at most 4 in n,c,r and 7 in the coefficient index j. For center
`j=t+6`, `n=s+t+7`, the three coefficients precede the first shifted block.
The six exact gap expansions, after strict Farey chamber substitutions,
each have 3,645 nonnegative rational coefficients in the nonnegative slacks.
The complete certificates are retained. Universal normalization, coefficient
extraction, certificate checking, boundary positions, later blocks and small
cycles remain separate obligations. This is a proposed uniform route, not
a new universal fourth-slice inequality.
