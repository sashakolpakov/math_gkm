# Scope and source correspondence for request 6d34

Machine-authored scope record; not the independent hash-bound completion audit.
Neither the exact root nor its negation is proved.

1. Coverage. New source inequalities concern every repeated-right path, with
   no path-length bound, and all valid positive cut counts and consecutive
   intervals of their active degrees. They do not cover every reduced rational.
   Inherited coverage results are reused only where explicitly imported; the
   older independent audit is historical and is not claimed to certify new bytes.
2. Source family. central_right0618 identifies the actual source word. The
   prefix formula uses sourceTerms_coeffFe01 and the inherited exact raw Pell
   sum. The new mask enumeration retains every mask with its original weight.
   The full arbitrary-word and general mechanical-word claims remain open.
3. Homogeneity and coefficients. The cut coordinate is i+j=a+c. The degree
   marker records an individual mask's y-polynomial degree; it is not the
   omitted homogeneous variable w. Degree layers and their interval sums are
   auxiliary summands of the original raw slice. No binomial, factorial or
   probabilistic coefficient normalization occurs. The real-to-integer
   transport is coefficientwise via Polynomial.map, not numerical evaluation.
4. Polygon. The new layer inequalities hold for all natural coefficient indices.
   They concern sub-sums of a fixed slice; they do not replace the pinned
   polygon by an auxiliary support region. No claimed counterexample is made.
   The protected InPolygon predicate and inherited outside-support bridges
   remain unchanged.
5. Directions, zeros and endpoints. Varying y within a fixed cut count is the
   original constant-i+j direction. Horizontal comparisons vary cut count;
   vertical comparisons vary both cut count and y degree. Those universal
   comparisons are not supplied by this work. Empty tails remain zero; no
   support entries are filtered out. The root's separate 0/1 and 1/1 clauses
   are untouched and are not replaced by the repeated-right subfamily theorem.
6. Assumptions. Accepted builds must have only propext, Classical.choice and
   Quot.sound in printed theorem closures. Failed elaborations may print an
   error-placeholder axiom; such builds are rejected and retained as failures.
   No explicit admission, custom axiom, native_decide or package update is used.
7. Proof scope. The contribution is exact higher-cut degree-interval aggregation
   for the repeated-B source words. It is not a proof of all source diagonals,
   full Pell WeakLC, or the full pinned Conjecture63. Finite experiments cannot
   fill those gaps. Validation reuses inherited compiled imports and separately
   replays new modules; it is not a fresh whole-source rebuild or an independent
   English/source audit. completion must remain null.
